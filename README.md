Project Title: ExploreLocal AI – Discover the Hidden Gems of Your City
Brief Project Description
ExploreLocal AI is an AI-powered local tourism and discovery platform designed to help people explore the hidden beauty, culture, history, and food of nearby destinations. It helps users discover lesser-known tourist attractions, local markets, historical places, traditional dishes, and unique experiences that are often overlooked by regular travel guides.

The platform provides personalized travel recommendations based on users' interests, budget, and available time. It also helps generate customized travel itineraries, estimate trip expenses, discover authentic local food through its Hidden Bites feature, save favourite places, and access directions through Google Maps.

The main aim of ExploreLocal AI is to promote local tourism, support small local businesses, and make discovering new places easier, smarter, and more enjoyable through AI technology.

In simple words: ExploreLocal AI acts as a smart travel companion that helps users explore more, spend wisely, discover hidden places, and experience the real culture of a destination.
1. What Is the Idea Behind ExploreLocal AI?
The idea behind ExploreLocal AI is to make local tourism smarter, easier, and more accessible with the help of Artificial Intelligence. The project is based on the concept of discovering places beyond popular tourist attractions.

Many cities and regions have beautiful hidden destinations, historical places, traditional food, local markets, and cultural experiences that people often don't know about. ExploreLocal AI brings all these experiences together on one platform.

It uses AI-based recommendations to help users discover places according to their interests, budget, and available time. It also helps them plan trips, explore local food, and experience the authentic culture of a destination.
2. What Problem Does ExploreLocal AI Solve?
Traditional travel platforms mainly focus on famous tourist destinations. Because of this, travellers may miss many interesting local places and authentic experiences.

ExploreLocal AI aims to solve the following problems:

1. Lack of Information About Hidden Places: People often struggle to find lesser-known tourist attractions and hidden gems in their area.

2. Difficulty in Travel Planning: Planning a trip according to budget, interests, and available time can be confusing and time-consuming.

3. Limited Knowledge of Local Culture: Tourists may not know about traditional dishes, historical stories, local markets, and cultural experiences.

4. Scattered Travel Information: Information about destinations, food, budgets, and directions is often available across different platforms.

5. Lack of Personalized Recommendations: Many travel platforms provide general suggestions rather than recommendations based on individual preferences.

6. Limited Promotion of Local Businesses: Small local shops, food vendors, and lesser-known attractions may not receive enough visibility among tourists.
3. Our Proposed Solution
ExploreLocal AI provides a single platform where users can discover hidden destinations, explore local food, receive personalized AI recommendations, generate customized itineraries, estimate travel expenses, and get directions through Google Maps.

Its goal is to connect travellers with local culture while promoting lesser-known destinations and supporting local businesses.
